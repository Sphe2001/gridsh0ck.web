About PowerPulse

Why we built this

A single connected system for the whole outage lifecycle — not another disconnected spreadsheet or call centre.

The City of Tshwane needs a single, real-time system to manage service outages from the moment they're reported until they're resolved. Today, information can go stale because residents, dispatchers, electricians and municipal teams don't always share one connected platform — which leads to delays, poor communication and repairs that are hard to track.

PowerPulse is a working prototype of that connected platform. It takes an outage from report to resolution, and turns every closed job into historical data the City can use to spot recurring problems — before they escalate into costly, repeat failures.

4 Dispatch & assign
